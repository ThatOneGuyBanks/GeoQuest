DAY TRIPPING QUIZ
BEFORE YOU EXPLORE

Adventure responsibly.

Day Tripping Quiz does its best to work out where you are, but phone GPS is not magic and can sometimes be inaccurate.

  • Stay on public paths and never enter private property.
  • Do not cross unsafe roads, climb barriers, enter water, or put yourself in danger.
  • Look around you and use common sense. The app should never be more important than your safety.
  • Children should explore with a responsible adult.

Be sensible, keep your eyes open, and have fun.
