Day Tripping Quiz
PLAIN-ENGLISH PRIVACY

Your adventure stays yours.

Day Tripping Quiz has no accounts, advertising or analytics. Progress is stored in your browser. This page explains the few network requests the app makes and what “offline” means.

Information kept on your device

Adventure progress, scores, achievements, preferences, safety acknowledgement and the last open screen are stored locally in your browser. Saved adventures and app files may also be held in the browser cache for offline use. Nothing in that local record is sent to Day Tripping Quiz.

You can export a JSON backup, reset progress from Help & settings, or clear this site’s browser data. Clearing browser data removes local progress and offline copies unless you have exported a backup.

Location

Location access is requested only when you choose a feature that needs it, such as nearby sorting or checking a discovery. Coordinates and GPS accuracy are compared with route stops inside your browser; Day Tripping Quiz does not receive or store them on a server.

Device and browser permission controls decide whether location is available. You can refuse or revoke permission at any time.

Photos, notes and sharing

Discovery photos, field-note text and postcard messages remain in memory for the current adventure. They are not uploaded or saved to browser storage. They leave the app only when you explicitly use your device’s share or download action.

Third-party services

The app loads the Leaflet map library from unpkg and map tiles from OpenStreetMap. Those providers receive normal web-request information such as your IP address, browser details and the requested resource or map-tile area. Day Tripping Quiz does not control their logs.

Directions, venue-hours sources, Apple Maps and Google Maps open only when you choose their links. The destination and the usual request information are then handled by that provider under its own privacy terms.

Offline use

The app shell is cached after a successful online visit. You can save an individual adventure’s clues for later. Saved clues, scoring and progress work offline; live map tiles, external directions, venue websites and links require a connection. A small, bounded set of recently viewed map tiles may remain cached, but complete offline maps are not promised.

Questions or problems

Use the project’s GitHub issue form. Please do not include precise personal locations, private photos, backup files or other sensitive information in a public issue.
