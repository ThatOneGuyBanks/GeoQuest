# Privacy and offline use

Day Tripping Quiz has no accounts, advertising or analytics. Adventure progress, scores, preferences, safety acknowledgement and cached offline files stay in the player's browser.

- Location is requested only for nearby sorting, directions preparation or discovery checks. Coordinates and accuracy are processed in the browser and are not sent to a Day Tripping Quiz server.
- Discovery photos, field notes and postcard messages remain in memory for the current adventure. They leave the app only when the player explicitly shares or downloads the resulting image.
- Leaflet is loaded from unpkg and map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap. Those third parties receive ordinary web-request information. Apple Maps, Google Maps and venue websites are opened only after the player chooses a link.
- Saved clues, scoring and progress can work offline. Live maps, external directions, venue websites and other external links require a connection.
- Players can export a progress backup, reset their progress in the app or clear the site's browser data.

The complete player-facing notice is published at <https://thatoneguybanks.github.io/GeoQuest/privacy.html>.
