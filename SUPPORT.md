# Support

Report reproducible problems through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ThatOneGuyBanks/GeoQuest/issues/new).

Please include:

- the device and browser;
- whether the app was installed or opened in a browser tab;
- whether the device was online, offline or on patchy signal;
- the screen and action immediately before the problem;
- a screenshot when it does not expose private information.

Do not post precise personal locations, discovery photos, progress-backup files or other sensitive information in a public issue.

For route-content or route-verification feedback, follow the project owner's route-review process rather than changing pack data in a general bug report.
